帮我解释一下Deal Flow: Problem Tracks
These four tracks come straight from problems NYC VCs and LPs told us they would pay to solve. Pick one, talk to the people who have the problem before build day, and build a company, not a project.
Track 1: Sourcing. Find the founder before the round
Problem statement. The best deals are won before a company raises. Today most funds find founders through warm intros and the same LinkedIn searches every other fund runs, so they all chase the same names at the same time. Build a system that surfaces exceptional founders early, from public signals, and explains why each one deserves a meeting.
Who has this problem: associates and partners at pre-seed and seed funds, venture scouts, angel syndicates.
Directions to explore
A founder-quality score built from public signals: shipped projects, GitHub activity, papers, hackathon wins, job changes
Alerts when a strong operator leaves a top company or a new company files a Form D
A one-page "why this founder" brief for every lead: what they built, the shared connections, which part of the thesis they fit
Thesis matching that ranks inbound and outbound leads against a fund's stated focus
Why it can be a company: every fund already pays for sourcing data. The opening is better signal and a clear explanation for each lead, not more names.
Data to start with: SEC EDGAR (Form D filings), the GitHub API, arXiv, Crunchbase, and the Akta API from Wokelo. LinkedIn's terms prohibit scraping, so use official APIs, data people export with consent, or licensed providers.
Ask in your interviews
What share of your last 10 deals came from outbound?
What signal got the last great founder a meeting with you?
What would make you trust a score enough to act on it?
Track 2: Diligence. Underwrite a startup in an hour, not two weeks
Problem statement. VCs told us the three things that matter most in diligence: a startup's market signals, its comps, and the issues hiding in its financials and metrics. A first pass still takes an associate days of pulling data, rebuilding numbers from a messy data room and spotting what does not add up. Build a tool that takes a deck or data room and returns the market signals, the right comps and the red flags a partner needs before a second meeting.
Who has this problem: associates and principals at VC funds, angels doing diligence alone, accelerators screening hundreds of applicants.
Directions to explore
A red-flag engine for financials and metrics: churn hidden in cohort charts, revenue counted as ARR when it is not recurring, burn that does not match the hiring plan, numbers that disagree between the deck and the model
A comp builder that picks comparable companies and recent rounds, and explains why each one is a fair comp
A market signal tracker for a company and its competitors: hiring, web traffic, reviews, pricing changes, customer wins
A first-draft investment memo where every claim links back to its source
Why it can be a company: diligence is expensive time for every fund, angel group and corporate VC. A tool a partner trusts on the first pass saves days on every deal.
Data to start with: public company filings on SEC EDGAR for comps, the Akta API from Wokelo, Crunchbase, and web search APIs such as You.com and Tavily. Bring a synthetic data room; never upload a real company's confidential data.
Ask in your interviews
What do you check first when you open a data room?
Which red flag did you once miss and regret?
How long does your first-pass memo take, and which part is the slowest?
Track 3: Portfolio. Know every company before the board meeting
Problem statement. A fund with 40 portfolio companies gets 40 updates in 40 formats, when it gets them at all. Partners often learn a company is in trouble when it asks for a bridge. LPs who invest across many funds see even less: the same sector bet can sit in five of their GPs at once. Build the layer that turns scattered portfolio data into early warnings and patterns a fund can act on.
Who has this problem: platform and portfolio teams at VC funds, partners, fund-of-funds and LPs invested with many GPs.
Directions to explore
Agentic check-ins: an agent that asks founders for updates, reads the replies, follows up on gaps and flags who needs help
A firm-wide, open-source model on a fund's own portfolio data, so partners can question their portfolio in plain language while the data stays in-house
A cross-GP view for LPs that combines portfolio data from many funds to show sector concentration, overlap and trends
A runway and risk radar: projected cash-out dates and early warning signs across the whole portfolio
Why it can be a company: every fund tracks its portfolio, and most still do it in spreadsheets and inboxes. A self-hosted, open-source option answers the biggest objection funds raise: their data cannot leave the firm.
Data to start with: a synthetic portfolio of 20 to 50 companies with monthly updates (generate it before the event), open-weight models you can run yourself, and the Akta API from Wokelo for outside signals on each company.
Ask in your interviews
How do you collect founder updates today, and what share of companies reply?
What do you wish you had known three months earlier about a company that struggled?
Which portfolio data would you never let leave the firm?
Track 4: Funds. Make LPs as informed as their GPs
Problem statement. LPs commit capital for ten years and get PDFs in return. Yearly K-1s and quarterly reports arrive in different formats, valuation marks move with little explanation, and judging a GP's real track record means rebuilding it by hand. Secondary buyers face the same fog when they price an LP stake. Build tools that read fund documents, diligence GP teams and price what LPs own.
Who has this problem: family offices, endowments, fund-of-funds, emerging LPs, secondary buyers, fund admins, and emerging managers raising Fund I or II.
Directions to explore
A K-1 and quarterly report reader that pulls out NAV, capital calls, distributions and valuation changes, and explains what moved since last quarter
GP diligence: rebuild a manager's track record, sourcing ability and key metrics (TVPI, DPI, IRR) from public and shared data
Secondary pricing: estimate what an LP stake is worth from the underlying NAV, the portfolio's latest marks and current market discounts
One dashboard of exposure, cash flows and performance across every fund an LP holds
Why it can be a company: GPs have a full stack of software, while most LPs still run on spreadsheets and email. Every LP gets these documents every quarter, so a tool that reads them is used every quarter.
Data to start with: adviser filings (Form ADV) on the SEC's adviser search, the reporting templates published by ILPA, and synthetic K-1s and quarterly reports you generate before the event. Never upload real LP documents without the owner's permission.
Ask in your interviews
How many hours does one quarter of fund reports take your team?
How do you judge a GP's track record today, and what can't you verify?
How do you decide what an LP stake is worth on the secondary market?

这四个 Track 本质上对应 VC/LP 工作流里的四个不同环节：**找项目 → 判断项目 → 管已投项目 → 管基金本身**。区别不只是功能不同，而是用户、数据、商业模式和 hackathon 的实现难度都不一样。

| Track | 一句话理解 | 主要用户 | 你最终要做出的产品 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Sourcing** | 帮 VC 更早发现值得投的创始人 | Seed VC、Scout、Angel | “Founder discovery + ranking” 系统 |
| **2. Diligence** | 帮 VC 快速判断一个项目值不值得继续看 | Associate、Principal、Angel | AI 尽调助手 |
| **3. Portfolio** | 帮 VC 持续监控已经投的公司 | Portfolio team、Partner、LP | Portfolio intelligence dashboard |
| **4. Funds** | 帮 LP 判断基金和 GP 表现 | Family office、FoF、Endowment、Secondary buyer | LP / Fund analytics 平台 |

### Track 1：Sourcing —— 在融资发生之前找到好创始人

现在大多数 VC 找项目主要依赖熟人介绍、LinkedIn、Crunchbase，所以很多基金最后看到的是**同一批公司**。这个 Track 想解决的是：

> 能不能通过公开数据，在一个创业者正式融资之前，就发现“这个人可能要创业，而且可能很强”？

例如系统发现一个 Google AI 工程师最近离职，同时 GitHub 活跃度突然增加，新建了几个 repository，又在 arXiv 发了论文。你的系统可以判断：

**Founder Score: 87/100**

原因可能是：

- 前 OpenAI / Google / Stripe 员工
- 最近离职
- GitHub 最近三个月活跃度明显增加
- 曾参加 YC / hackathon
- 研究方向符合基金的 AI infrastructure thesis

然后自动生成：

> **Why this founder?**
> Strong technical founder signal, recently left Google DeepMind, building open-source inference infrastructure, closely aligned with our AI infra thesis.

真正的产品不是“创业公司数据库”，而是：

**Signal detection → founder ranking → thesis matching → investment lead generation**

最关键的问题是：**为什么 VC 要相信你的评分？**

Hackathon 中最容易出现的问题是做成一个“漂亮的 Crunchbase 搜索器”，但没有真正产生独特 signal。

### Track 2：Diligence —— 把两周尽调压缩到一小时

这是 VC 收到 startup pitch deck 后发生的事情。

现在 Associate 可能需要：

看 deck → 查竞争对手 → 查市场 → 检查财务 → 看 ARR → 算 burn → 查融资 → 找异常 → 写 investment memo。

可能要几天。

这个 Track 的目标是：

> 上传 startup deck / data room，系统自动告诉投资人：这个公司怎么样，有什么问题，下一步应该问什么。

例如你上传一家 SaaS startup：

系统自动发现：

> **Red Flag 1 — ARR inconsistency**
> Pitch deck says ARR = $4.2M, but monthly recurring revenue implies approximately $3.5M ARR.

> **Red Flag 2 — Churn risk**
> Revenue grew 35%, but customer cohort retention declined from 92% to 76%.

> **Comparable companies**
> Company A
> Company B
> Company C

然后生成：

**First-pass Investment Memo**

包括：

Market
Competition
Traction
Unit economics
Risks
Questions for founders

而且每一个结论都有 source。

所以它不是普通的“AI summarizer”，而是：

**Data room → structured analysis → red flags → comps → memo**

这是四个 Track 中最容易让 VC 一眼看懂价值的方向之一。

### Track 3：Portfolio —— 在董事会之前知道哪个公司快出问题了

VC 投完钱以后还有一个很大的问题：

假设一个基金投了 **40家公司**。

每家公司每个月可能发：

PDF
Excel
Email
Notion
Google Sheet

甚至有些公司根本不发 update。

Partner 很容易直到 founder 说：

> “我们只剩 3 个月 runway，需要 bridge round。”

才发现公司已经出问题。

所以这个 Track 想做：

**Portfolio Operating System**

把所有公司信息统一进来：

Revenue
Burn
Runway
Hiring
Fundraising
Product
Customer
Risk

然后给 VC 一个 dashboard：

| Company | Runway | Revenue Growth | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Startup A | 18 months | +32% | Low |
| Startup B | 7 months | +5% | Medium |
| Startup C | 3 months | -12% | High |

系统甚至可以主动：

> “Startup C 的 runway 从 8 个月下降到 3 个月，同时 hiring freeze，建议 partner 联系 founder。”

这里特别适合做 **Agentic AI**：

Agent 自动给 founder 发 update request → founder 回复 → Agent 分析 → 发现缺失信息 → 自动 follow-up → 更新 portfolio dashboard。

所以核心是：

**Portfolio data → monitoring → anomaly detection → proactive intervention**

相比 Track 2，这是更长期、更 sticky 的 SaaS 产品，因为基金会一直用。

### Track 4：Funds —— 帮 LP 看懂自己到底投了什么

这个 Track 的用户不是普通 VC，而是 **LP**。

VC 是 GP。

例如：

Harvard Endowment → 投钱给 Sequoia Fund → Sequoia 再投 startup。

Harvard 就是 LP。

LP 最大的问题是，它可能投了：

20 个 VC funds。

每个 fund 每季度发：

PDF
K-1
Quarterly Report
Capital Call Notice

格式完全不同。

LP 最后可能靠 Excel 管理。

例如：

Sequoia：AI 35%
a16z：AI 40%
General Catalyst：AI 30%

LP 自己可能不知道：

> 原来我整个 portfolio 已经有 45% exposure 在 AI。

你的系统可以自动读取所有 Fund report，然后形成：

**LP Portfolio Dashboard**

显示：

NAV
Capital Calls
Distributions
TVPI
DPI
IRR
Sector Exposure
Fund overlap

甚至分析：

> “你的五个 VC funds 中，有三个都投资了 OpenAI ecosystem companies，因此实际 AI exposure 高于你认为的水平。”

或者做 **Secondary Pricing**：

LP 想卖掉某个 VC Fund stake。

系统估算：

> NAV = $10M
> Market discount = 18%
> Estimated secondary value ≈ $8.2M

这是一个很真实的金融科技问题，但相对更专业，需要理解 fund accounting、K-1、NAV、TVPI、DPI、IRR。

---

如果从 hackathon 的角度看，我会这样理解四个题：

**Track 1 = VC 版 LinkedIn / Bloomberg signal discovery**

**Track 2 = VC 版 AI investment analyst**

**Track 3 = VC 版 Datadog / CRM for portfolio companies**

**Track 4 = Bloomberg / Carta for LPs**

结合你们现在团队背景，也就是**一级市场投资经历 + SDE/CS + 产品/UX + 还准备找一个 VC 背景成员**，我认为优先级是：

**Track 2 > Track 1 > Track 3 > Track 4。**

Track 2 最适合 9 小时 hackathon，因为输入和输出都非常清楚：

**Pitch deck → AI diligence → red flags + comps + investment memo**

Demo 也很强。你可以直接在台上上传一个 startup deck，然后几秒以后出现：

> “We found 4 inconsistencies investors should investigate before the second meeting.”

这比展示一个普通 dashboard 更容易形成明显的 **before / after**。

Track 1 也非常适合做，但真正困难的是建立可信的 **founder scoring logic**。Track 3 商业价值很好，但 9 小时内容易做成普通 dashboard。Track 4 的市场很真实，但 domain knowledge 门槛最高，而且 LP workflow 对普通评委来说通常没有 Track 2 那么直观。

如果你明确想做 **SaaS 平台 + Multi-Agent AI**，同时又想抓一个很小、很具体、9 小时内能做深的问题，我建议不要做“完整 VC diligence 平台”，而是切到一个单点工作流：

**“Deck–Model Consistency Checker”——自动检查融资材料里不同文件和页面之间的数据是否自相矛盾。**

这是 Track 2 里非常小但很真实的问题。VC 经常会同时看到 pitch deck、financial model、KPI sheet、cohort chart、hiring plan。真正费时间的不是总结，而是发现：

- Deck 写 ARR = $4.2M，但 Excel 里 MRR × 12 只有 $3.6M
- Deck 写 gross margin = 78%，model 算出来只有 69%
- Founder 说 runway 18 months，但现金余额和 burn rate 对不上
- Headcount plan 显示未来半年新增 15 人，但 burn forecast 几乎没变化
- 一页写客户数 120，另一页写 147
- “Recurring revenue”里面实际上混入了一次性 implementation fee

这个点很适合 Multi-Agent，因为每个 agent 可以承担一个非常明确的职责，而不是为了 multi-agent 而 multi-agent：

**Agent 1 — Extraction Agent**
从 deck、Excel、PDF 中抽取所有关键指标，并统一成标准 schema。

**Agent 2 — Reconciliation Agent**
把同一个指标在不同文件和不同页面中的数值进行 cross-check。

**Agent 3 — Finance Agent**
重新计算 ARR、growth、burn multiple、runway、gross margin 等指标。

**Agent 4 — Skeptic Agent**
专门寻找“ technically true but misleading”的表述，比如 ARR 定义、customer count、churn presentation。

**Agent 5 — Evidence Agent**
把每一个 flag 绑定到具体 source，告诉投资人：
“Deck page 8 says X, financial model cell B17 implies Y.”

最后 SaaS 页面不要做复杂，可以只做三个核心区域：

**Documents → Flags → Evidence**

例如用户上传三个文件以后，系统直接输出：

> **High Severity — ARR mismatch**
> Pitch deck reports $5.1M ARR.
> Financial model implies $4.3M recurring annualized revenue.
> Difference: 18.6%.

然后下面给：

**Why this matters**
ARR may include non-recurring implementation revenue.

**Ask the founder**
“What portion of reported ARR comes from recurring subscription contracts?”

这个产品的价值会非常清楚：

**不是帮 VC 写 memo，而是在 second meeting 前帮他发现应该问什么。**

我认为这个切口甚至比“完整 AI diligence copilot”更强，因为它有三个优势。

第一，**痛点非常具体**。评委一听就懂：“投资人不想被漂亮 deck 里的数字骗。”

第二，**AI 是必要的，而不是装饰性的**。你需要 document understanding、cross-document reasoning、financial calculation 和 evidence tracing。

第三，**非常容易 demo**。你可以故意准备一个 synthetic startup data room，其中埋 5 个问题。现场上传以后，让系统逐个找出来。

如果你想再更小一点，我会考虑下面三个方向，但首选仍然是第一个：

| Micro problem | 产品 | 推荐度 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **数字自相矛盾** | Deck–Model Consistency Checker | **★★★★★** |
| Runway 算错 | Runway & Burn Auditor | ★★★★ |
| ARR 定义有问题 | SaaS Metrics Integrity Checker | ★★★★ |
| Comps 选得不合理 | Comparable Company Critic | ★★★ |
| Memo 没有 source | Evidence-backed Memo Validator | ★★★ |

你们甚至可以把产品定位写成一句很强的话：

> **“An AI red-team for startup data rooms.”**

或者更商业化一点：

> **“Catch the numbers that don’t add up before your second meeting.”**

如果考虑后续真正做成公司，我会建议从这个小工具开始，然后逐渐扩成：

**Consistency Checker → Financial Red Flags → Market Signals → Full Diligence OS**

这样 hackathon 做的是一个非常小的 wedge，但商业叙事可以很大。

如果限定在**医疗行业**，我反而更建议你不要做“Healthcare VC 全流程尽调平台”，而是抓一个医疗投资中特别典型、普通 VC 工具解决不了的问题：**公司在 Deck 里讲的临床价值，是否真的被它引用的临床证据支持。**

我最推荐的切口是：

## Clinical Claim Validator

**“AI red-team for healthcare startup claims.”**

医疗 startup 的特殊之处在于，一家公司可以同时说：

> “Our AI detects disease earlier.”
> “Clinically validated.”
> “FDA cleared.”
> “Reduces hospitalizations by 30%.”
> “Reimbursable by Medicare.”

这些话看起来都很好，但实际上可能分别对应完全不同的证据。医疗投资尽调需要同时判断 clinical evidence、regulatory pathway、reimbursement 和 workflow adoption，而且 FDA authorization 本身并不意味着临床价值或 reimbursement 已经被证明。[Clinical Ventures](https://clinical.ventures/blog/healthcare-startup-due-diligence-physician-investor-framework-v2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

所以你的产品只解决一个问题：

> **这家医疗 startup 在 Pitch Deck 里说的话，证据到底够不够？**

### 一个具体 Demo

VC 上传一家医疗 AI startup 的 Pitch Deck。

Deck 里面写：

> **“Our AI reduces 30-day hospital readmission by 35%.”**

你的 SaaS 自动做：

**Agent 1 — Claim Extraction**

识别所有 clinical claims：

- “35% reduction in readmission”
- “92% diagnostic accuracy”
- “FDA cleared”
- “Medicare reimbursable”
- “Validated across 5 hospitals”

然后形成一个 **Claim Ledger**。

---

**Agent 2 — Evidence Retrieval**

针对每一个 claim 自动去找对应 evidence：

ClinicalTrials.gov
PubMed / papers
FDA databases
CMS / reimbursement documentation
Company publications

例如找到论文以后发现：

> Study: retrospective
> N = 126
> Single hospital
> Primary endpoint = model AUROC
> No prospective intervention study

---

**Agent 3 — Evidence Critic**

真正关键的是这个 Agent。

它比较：

**Company claim**

> “Reduces readmission by 35%.”

和：

**Actual evidence**

> Retrospective model predicts readmission with AUROC 0.82.

然后 flag：

> 🔴 **Unsupported clinical utility claim**

原因：

> The published study evaluates predictive accuracy, but does not demonstrate that using the model reduces hospital readmission.

这就是一个非常典型的医疗 AI 问题：

**prediction ≠ improved clinical outcome**

---

**Agent 4 — Regulatory Checker**

Deck 写：

> “FDA Approved”

但 FDA database 实际可能显示：

> 510(k) Cleared

系统直接指出：

> 🟠 **Regulatory wording mismatch**

并解释：

> The product is FDA-cleared through 510(k), not FDA-approved.

对于医疗投资人，这种差别很重要。Healthcare diligence 里 regulatory terminology、authorized labeling 和实际 commercial claim 是否一致，本身就是重点检查项。[Clinical Ventures](https://clinical.ventures/blog/healthcare-startup-due-diligence-physician-investor-framework-v2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

**Agent 5 — Investor Question Generator**

最后不是写一个巨大的 memo，而是生成：

### Questions before the next meeting

**Clinical**

> Has the claimed 35% reduction in readmissions been demonstrated prospectively?

**Generalizability**

> Has the model been externally validated outside the original health system?

**Regulatory**

> Is readmission reduction included within the FDA-authorized intended use?

**Workflow**

> What clinical action is triggered when the model identifies a high-risk patient?

VC 马上知道第二次 meeting 应该问什么。

**61 / 100**

## 为什么我认为它比普通 VC Diligence 更适合你们

你们真正可以形成差异化的地方不是“我们也用了 Multi-Agent”，而是：

> **General VC AI understands companies.
> We understand clinical evidence.**

现在已经存在专门向 healthcare investors 提供 clinical diligence、literature validation、reimbursement 和 adoption risk assessment 的服务，这至少说明这类 diligence 本身确实有人付费。[Kurbside Partners](https://kurbside.co/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

而医疗创业公司的商业风险也确实不只是 revenue 和 TAM。产品即使技术好、甚至完成监管路径，也可能因为 clinical adoption、workflow、reimbursement 等问题失败。[digital.health](https://digital.health/digital-health-startup-guide?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

因此你可以把整个商业故事讲成：

**Today**

Healthcare VC associate：

Pitch deck
↓
Google

所以核心价值是：

> **Find founders before they appear in traditional VC databases—and explain why they matter.**

这个方向很适合你们比赛。

---

### 2. Federated Diligence：更有技术壁垒，但 Hackathon 难度更高

这个想法解决的是另一个很好的问题：

VC 做 diligence 时，公司不愿意把：

customer-level data
patient data
proprietary metrics
transaction data

全部交给投资人。

于是出现矛盾：

> **VC 想验证数字，但 startup 不想泄露底层数据。**

你提到 Rhino Health / federated learning 的思路可以借鉴：

**数据不离开企业环境，验证逻辑进入企业环境。**

例如 startup 声称：

> NRR = 124%

VC 不需要获得完整 customer table。

验证程序在 startup 环境运行：

**Raw customer data**
→ local calculation
→ 输出：

> NRR = 121.7%
> Claim verification: ⚠️ Mismatch

VC 只看到验证结果和 methodology，不看到具体客户数据。

更准确的产品概念其实不是传统 federated learning，而是：

> **Privacy-Preserving Diligence**

或者：

> **Verify the metric, without seeing the data.**

这很有意思，但 9 小时内如果真的做 federated infrastructure，会明显增加工程风险。可以做 synthetic prototype，但不建议作为这次最主要的 MVP。

---

### 3. VC → PE：是另一个独立商业问题

你最后写的：

> IPO 行不通 → VC 卖给 PE

本质上是在考虑 **venture liquidity / secondary / exit intelligence**。

例如某 VC portfolio：

Startup A 已经 Series D
增长放缓
IPO window 不理想
ARR $80M
EBITDA 接近正数

Agent 自动判断：

> 这家公司可能已经不适合下一轮 traditional VC，但可能适合 growth PE / buyout。

然后寻找：

**哪些 PE fund 的 thesis、ticket size、sector、EBITDA requirement 与它匹配？**

形成：

> Company → Exit Readiness → Potential PE Buyers

这是不错的产品，但已经和前两个方向完全不同，不建议放在同一个 Hackathon 产品中。

---

### 如果现在三个方向选一个

我会排序：

**① Founder Signal Agent > ② Privacy-Preserving Diligence > ③ VC-to-PE Exit Intelligence**

第一名最适合这次比赛，因为 problem statement 已经明确要求：

> **“Find the founder before the round.”**

而你们可以把它进一步形成一个非常具体的 wedge：

> **Find technical founders emerging from universities and research labs before they start fundraising.**

技术结构也非常清楚：

**GitHub + Papers + University/Lab + Hackathons**
→ **Signal Agents**
→ **Founder Identity Resolution**
→ **Explainable Founder Score**
→ **Fund Thesis Matching**
→ **Alert**

最后 Demo 不需要复杂：

> “昨天这个人还只是 Cornell/Stanford/MIT researcher；今天系统发现 GitHub、research commercialization 和 career signals 同时发生变化，因此在他进入 Crunchbase 之前把他推给 VC。”

这个项目与前面的 Healthcare Diligence 最好作为**两个候选方案分别比较**，而不是强行合并。
